    The circle of fifths doesn't quite close.

Sources:
Pythagorean tuning stacks pure 3:2 fifths. Twelve of them overshoot seven octaves by the Pythagorean comma, (3/2)^12 / 2^7 ≈ 1.0136.
Equal temperament spreads that error evenly across the twelve semitones, which is why no interval but the octave is pure.
